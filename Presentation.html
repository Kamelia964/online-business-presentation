Global: Sell to millions from your laptop.

1. Market Research

Before selling, you must find a problem that people want to solve.

Check the competition.
Identify your "ideal" customer.
Find a unique product gap.

There are many ways to make money online. Here are the top three:

E-Commerce

Selling physical goods online.

SaaS / Apps

Providing tools or digital services.

Digital Goods

Selling courses or e-books.

3. The Setup

Domain: Choose a catchy brand name.
Platform: Use Shopify or WordPress.
Payments: Connect PayPal or Stripe.

4. Get Customers

SEO: Rank high on Google search.
Social Media: Use Instagram & TikTok.
Consistency: Post every day to build trust.
